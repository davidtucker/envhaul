# Code of Conduct

envhaul is a technical project. Keep every interaction professional,
constructive and kind.

## Expected

- Be respectful, and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Be patient with questions and with people new to the project.
- Stay on topic in issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults or harassment.
- Threats, or sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests
and discussions) and wherever you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions,
and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks this
code, at their discretion.
